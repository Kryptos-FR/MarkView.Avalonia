# Streaming with **open** `syntax` and [a link](https://example.com/heading "Heading") in a heading

Every paragraph on this page ends its first line inside a construct, so **an update that cuts
the line** renders it healed.

A code span starts here and the backticks `stay open across the
line break` until the next one.

The address of [the streaming
guide](https://example.com/docs/streaming?part=healing "The guide") is cut by most chunks.

Strikethrough opens with two tildes: ~~this was the old
plan~~ and this is the new one.

Underscores open emphasis between words: _this is
emphasised_, but snake_case_names stay literal.
